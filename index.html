Our Story

Where Bold Flavours
Meet Great Vibes.

We began as a modest tawa counter with one quiet obsession — flavour that lingers long after the plate is cleared. Today, Mumbai Tadka by Big Tawa is where Nashik gathers for slow-cooked curries, live tandoor smoke and generous hospitality.

Every masala is ground in-house, every kebab is fired to order, and every table is set the way we'd set it for family. No shortcuts, no compromise — only the honest taste of a kitchen that truly cares.
